# Dashboard surface brief

## Scope & mode
Local Software Factory operate console (`apps/dashboard`). Mode: Operate.

## Audience & job
Factory operator on a local/self-hosted stack. Create tasks, watch the fixed pipeline graph, cancel runs, rerun nodes.

## Constraints
Simple and functional. Keep incumbent dark clinical world (near-black surfaces, IBM Plex, status colors only). No auth UI, evidence browser, or redesign. Empty/loading/error states must teach the next action.

## Direction
Finish incumbent console (finish-incumbent). Memorable moment: live node pulse on the assembly-line graph while cancel/rerun stay one click away.

None for v1.
